# 5-Dimensional Tone of Voice Specification

## 1. Schema Definition

```markdown
# Persona Voice Profile: {Voice Identifier}

## 1. Syntactic Pacing (句式与节奏)
- **Sentence Length Variation**: Short punchy fragments vs long analytical clauses.
- **Rhetorical Cadence**: Frequency of questions, parallelisms, and rhythmic breaks.

## 2. Lexical Palette (词汇偏好)
- **Signature Vocabulary**: Characteristic keywords, idiomatic terms.
- **Banned Words & Jargon**: List of forbidden corporate jargon and AI clichés.

## 3. Tonal & Rhetorical Attitude (态度与语气)
- **Perspective**: First-person subjective vs third-person objective.
- **Emotional Temperature**: Warm/provocative/analytical/pragmatic.

## 4. Formatting & Punctuation (排版习惯)
- **Paragraph Length**: Maximum sentences per paragraph.
- **Punctuation Preferences**: Dash, colon, list formatting style.

## 5. Negative Constraints (负向硬约束)
- Banned AI transition words and conclusions.
```
